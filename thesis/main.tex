\chapter*{Abstract}
In this study, a tiered artificial-intelligence classification system is proposed to distinguish pneumothorax from normal lung findings in chest radiographs. The proposed model presents a dynamic decision mechanism that routes low-confidence predictions to the next tier, using a MobileNetV2 (Tier 1) and an EfficientNet-B4 / Ark+ (Tier 2) architecture. Monte Carlo Dropout and Test-Time Augmentation are integrated for uncertainty analysis, and Conformal Prediction is used to provide statistical coverage guarantees for the predictions. Experimental results show that the proposed method improves accuracy while keeping the diagnostic error rate low and reducing the average computational cost.

\vspace{1cm}
\noindent \textbf{Keywords:} Chest Radiography, Pneumothorax Classification, Tiered Classification, Conformal Prediction, Monte Carlo Dropout.
