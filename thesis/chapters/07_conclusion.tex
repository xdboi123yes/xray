\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}

Within the scope of this bachelor's thesis, a tiered classification and uncertainty-based safe-routing system was successfully designed and validated for pneumothorax detection in chest radiographs.

The proposed two-stage model architecture provides a practical solution to two fundamental problems in the use of deep-learning models for medical imaging: high computational cost and the lack of uncertainty measurement. The comprehensive tests and ablation studies show that the system also achieves high generalizability across different datasets.

In conclusion, this Conformal-Prediction-backed, uncertainty-aware tiered classification approach offers a strong and innovative reference point for the design of the intelligent clinical-support systems of the future.
