\chapter{Experimental Setup}
\label{ch:experimental_setup}

This chapter describes the datasets, the hardware/software infrastructure, and the training parameters used in the experimental evaluation of this thesis.

\section{Datasets}
\subsection{NIH Chest X-Ray 14}
The NIH ChestX-Ray14 dataset \cite{wang2017chestxray} was used for pneumothorax detection. The radiographs were preprocessed with CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization) \cite{zuiderveld1994contrast} and normalized to the ImageNet statistics. The dataset was partitioned into train, validation, calibration and test splits; the split sizes and class balance are reported in Table \ref{tab:dataset_stats}. The task is markedly class-imbalanced, with pneumothorax present in roughly 16.7\% of the images across every split.

\subsection{CheXpert}
The Stanford CheXpert dataset \cite{irvin2019chexpert} was used to test the generalizability of the developed tiered system and to measure its zero-shot cross-dataset performance. A small out-of-domain probe (33 frontal radiographs, 7 pneumothorax-positive and 26 normal) was evaluated zero-shot, without any fine-tuning on CheXpert; the corresponding result is reported as configuration A14 in Chapter \ref{ch:results}.

\section{Hardware and Software Infrastructure}
% author: confirm the exact training hardware you used before final submission.
Model training and evaluation were carried out on NVIDIA GPUs provided by Google Colab (Tesla T4 and A100), with local development on Apple Silicon (MPS). The software stack used PyTorch 2.x, FastAPI 0.110.0, and React 18.3.1, running on Python 3.10. Experiments were tracked with MLflow.
