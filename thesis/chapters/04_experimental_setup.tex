\chapter{Experimental Setup}
\label{ch:experimental_setup}

This chapter describes the datasets, the hardware/software infrastructure, and the training parameters used in the experimental evaluation of this thesis.

\section{Datasets}
\subsection{NIH Chest X-Ray 14}
The NIH Chest X-Ray 14 dataset was used for pneumothorax detection. The radiographs were preprocessed with CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization) and normalization.

\subsection{CheXpert}
The Stanford CheXpert dataset was used to test the generalizability of the developed tiered system and to measure its zero-shot cross-dataset performance.

\section{Hardware and Software Infrastructure}
Training and testing were carried out on Apple M3 Max (MPS) and NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPU hardware. The software stack used PyTorch (v2.3.0), FastAPI (v0.111.0), and React (v18.3.1). Experiments were tracked with MLflow.
